# Phase 4 — Behavioral Patterns + Modern Patterns

**Why here:** machine-coding problems are mostly about *varying behaviour* (pricing, matching, dice,
eviction: Strategy), *lifecycles* (vending machine, order, elevator, booking: State), *reactions*
(notify on change: Observer), *undoable actions* (editor, transactions: Command/Memento) and
*pipelines of handlers* (approvals, ATM denominations, logger levels: Chain of Responsibility). Cycle 4.3
adds the patterns real product code uses constantly but GoF doesn't list: repository, specification,
null object, event bus, plugin, pipeline, rules engine. Needs Phases 1–3.
**Scaffolding:** hints on request only, after an honest attempt. Critiques now mix Phases 0–4.
**Reading (after the sessions):** HFDP ch 1 (Strategy), 2 (Observer), 6 (Command), 8 (Template Method),
10 (State), 12 (Compound patterns / MVC), 13 (Patterns in the real world); GoF ch 5 (behavioral catalogue).
